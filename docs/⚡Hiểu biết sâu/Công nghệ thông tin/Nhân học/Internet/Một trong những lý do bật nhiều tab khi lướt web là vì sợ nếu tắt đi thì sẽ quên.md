Khái niệm:: 
Kể cả khi biết là có thể dùng save for later thì cũng biết là khả năng là mình sẽ ko nhìn lại lần nữa
we fear we might lose something, so we save it in bookmarks. We then lose the fear of losing the thing and never look at those things again. Over time we learn that we don't really use bookmarks when there are too many of them, so we switch the focus to tabs: surely if I don't close the tab I won't forget I want to keep looking at it. But again they can fade pretty easily into the background after a while.
